House style: dialogue on its own line. Apply these rules whenever the source contains spoken dialogue.

- Convert every English quotation mark in speech to the Spanish raya (—, em dash U+2014), never a hyphen (-) or en dash (–). Leave no straight or curly English quotes in dialogue.
- TURN OPENING AND CONTINUATION: each spoken turn begins on its own new paragraph, opening with a raya attached directly to the first word, with NO space after it (—Hola., never — Hola.). Use » (U+00BB) ONLY when one speech paragraph is followed DIRECTLY by another speech paragraph by the SAME speaker (»Entonces…, never » Entonces…). If narration sits between them, resume with —. Also use — for every new speaker.
- Introductory or scene-setting narration goes in its OWN paragraph BEFORE the speech. Do not open a paragraph with narration and then attach the speech with a raya on the same line.
- Narrator comments during a turn are incisos, framed by rayas, inline: —Ese hombre —añadió— me ha engañado.
- NESTED SPEECH AND INTERNAL THOUGHTS: use guillemets « » (Paul pensó: «Hoy nacerá»), never English quotes.
